# Lessons for KOML

Exact score normalization and attention pair counting are different measurements. Pair-count reductions cannot establish runtime or model quality improvements. Boundary clipping changes local pair counts, so n*(2*w+1) alone overcounts the edges.

The planted fixture validates the assumption that a small CPU-only CLI can compute all declared outputs exactly and deterministically. It does not validate demand or research usefulness. Independent enumeration of local pairs and a mutated planted case guard against a plausible but incorrect formula and a hardcoded report.

Operating rule: define the measurable quantity and independent oracle before implementing a domain experiment. Preserve explicit inputs and provenance in the output, and postpone adoption verdicts until actual usage evidence exists.
